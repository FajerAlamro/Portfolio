Munir

AI-powered smart glasses & app for the visually impaired

The Idea

What is Munir?

Munir is an AI-powered assistive system that combines smart glasses with a Flutter mobile application to support visually impaired individuals. It uses OCR for text reading, custom AI models for face and Saudi Riyal currency recognition, OpenAI Vision for scene understanding, Firebase for backend services, and BLE with HTTPS/TLS for secure communication.

Privacy and security are central to the system. Facial images are protected with AES-256 encryption, biometric data is securely managed, and Firebase Authentication with strict security rules ensures authorized access only. Munir also provides GPS-based emergency location sharing, personalized reminders, and real-time voice feedback.

Munir was selected for the five-month IBM Developer Program, where the team received mentorship and technical guidance from IBM experts to refine the system architecture and enhance its AI-powered features.

How It Works

AI Models & Pipeline

Face Recognition

CAPTURE

Capture or upload 3-angle facial images for registration.

EMBED

Generate 512-dimensional embeddings with InsightFace.

SECURE

Encrypt thumbnails using AES-256-GCM and store them securely.

OUTPUT

Match the captured face with registered embeddings and announce the person's name.

Currency Identification

CAPTURE

Capture or upload a photo of a Saudi Riyal banknote.

PREPROCESS

Detect and crop the banknote, then preprocess the image for classification.

CLASSIFY

Classify the banknote using MobileNetV2 features and SVM (98.16% accuracy).

OUTPUT

Announce the identified currency value aloud.
