The Idea

What is Kasa?

Designed and implemented a relational database to organize and manage donation data. The project included database modeling, table creation, relationships, SQL queries, and data manipulation to support efficient data retrieval and analysis.

Supervisor view: managing donations, items, and charities

DATABASE STRUCTURE

Four Core Entities

Donor

Gives clothes a second life. One donor can give one to many items

DID · PK DFName DLName DPNO

Item

The donated piece itself, tagged, categorized, and dated the moment it's logged

IID · PK IName ICategory DODonation

Charity

An organization that receives donated items

CID · PK CName CLocation CContact

Supervisor

Supervises, views, and manages charities and donation items

SID · PK SEmailAddress SPNO SPassword
