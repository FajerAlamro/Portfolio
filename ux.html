Smart Cart

A UX case study for a smart cart that eliminates checkout lines

The Idea

What is Smart Cart?

Smart Cart redefines grocery shopping by making it faster, easier, and more convenient through automatic scanning, real-time calculations, and an intuitive interface.

Built on user research, it addresses common shopping challenges to deliver a seamless and enjoyable experience for shoppers of all ages.
