Wasl

A full-stack website connecting innovators with investors

The Idea

What is Wasl?

Many innovators struggle to present their ideas due to high website fees, while investors often struggle to discover promising projects. Wasl bridges this gap through a structured platform where innovators showcase ideas and investors explore opportunities using filtering and categorization.

Wasl was developed as a complete software engineering project, from requirements gathering and system design documentation to implementation, testing, and evaluation.

Two Sides, One Link

Roles in Wasl

Innovator

Brings the idea

  • Creates a free account and profile
  • Submits ideas with details, category, and media
  • Edits or removes listings anytime from "My Ideas"
  • Reviews investment requests and chooses who to accept
Investor

Finds the fit

  • Searches and browses the full idea catalog
  • Filters ideas by category and upload date
  • Saves promising ideas to an auto-updating favorites list
  • Requests to connect, then views contact info once accepted
On Every Account

Core Features
