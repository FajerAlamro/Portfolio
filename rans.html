Ransomware Attack

An educational video simulating a ransomware attack through a phishing email

The Idea

What is this project?

This project is an educational animation video simulating a ransomware attack triggered by a phishing email. It follows an employee who opens a malicious attachment, leading to file encryption and the spread of ransomware across the network.

The video also explains the impact of ransomware and highlights key practices to detect phishing attempts, prevent infections, and respond effectively to security incidents.

One click on a phishing email can encrypt an entire organization's files within minutes.

The Scenario

How it Unfolded

00:00 INFO

A phishing email arrives disguised as an urgent invoice request

00:14 INFO

The attachment is opened, a macro runs quietly in the background

00:41 WARNING

Files begin encrypting across the local machine

00:58 CRITICAL

A ransom note appears, demanding payment in cryptocurrency within 48 hours

01:20 CRITICAL

The infection spreads automatically to every connected device on the network

01:47 RESOLVED

Suspicious activity is verified and reported before any payment is made

02:10 RESOLVED

The lesson: prevention habits that stop this before step one

Staying Protected

Preventing Future Attacks

prevention_checklist.yaml STAY PROTECTED

$ cat prevention_checklist.yaml

verify_senders: check the actual address, not just the display name

disable_macros: never enable macros on an unexpected document

backup_offline: keep backups ransomware can't reach

segment_network: stop one infection from reaching everything

report_immediately: speed is what contains it
